# dates.md

Every date I am committed to or waiting on, so the brain can bring it to me on the day.

Format, one line per date:

    - YYYY-MM-DD | what happens | what to do or ask on the day | where it came from

To change a date, add a new line dated with the new date whose second field starts
"Correction to the <old date> <word> line:". Never delete the old one.
