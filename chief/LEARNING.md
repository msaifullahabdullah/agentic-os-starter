# LEARNING.md

How this brain improves itself. Learning does not need permission. Every line is dated, visible and reversible, which is the safety net.

A **fact** is something true about the owner's world. It goes to the pages.
A **learning** is something true about how things go: what worked, what failed, what the owner corrected, what they keep postponing, how they like to be taught. Learnings make the brain better rather than just bigger.

Where learnings go:
- About the owner's patterns: context/habits.md or identity/growth.md
- About how the assistant should behave: this file, under Learned
- About one agent's job: agents/<name>/wiki/learnings.md

Rules:
1. Only record what the evidence shows, with the date and what happened.
2. A pattern needs at least two dated examples before it is called a pattern.
3. When the owner corrects the assistant, that correction is recorded the same day.
