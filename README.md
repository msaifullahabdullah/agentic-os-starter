# Agentic OS

**A personal AI brain that remembers your life, keeps you on track, and gets better every week.**
It interviews you, fills itself in, and runs on your own accounts. Nobody else can see it.

---

## What you get

- **A brain that remembers.** People, dates, goals, habits, projects. Tell it once; it never forgets.
- **A daily dashboard.** What to do now, your three focus items, your daily habits, progress, and a close-the-day check in.
- **A morning briefing and an evening check in** on your phone.
- **It learns you.** It notices what works for you, what you keep putting off, and how you like to be told things.
- **Agents.** Say "make me an agent for…" and it builds one.
- **Private.** Your brain lives in your own GitHub. Only you can open it.

## What you need

- A free **GitHub** account: [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup)
- A paid **Claude** plan (Pro or above), for Claude Code

---

## Set up in 3 minutes

**1. Copy the brain**
Click **[Use this template](https://github.com/msaifullahabdullah/agentic-os-starter/generate)**. Name it **my-brain**, choose **Private**, click **Create repository**.

**2. Open it in Claude**
Go to **[claude.ai/code](https://claude.ai/code)**. Connect GitHub if asked, pick **my-brain**, and start a session.

**3. Say "set me up"**
Claude asks you about 8 short rounds of questions. Answer in your own words; say *skip* to skip.
It then fills in your whole brain and tells you what it knows.

That's it. Next, say **"publish my dashboard"** and **"set up my routines"**.

## Use it everywhere (10 minutes, optional)

To use your brain in any Claude chat, on your phone, and in your dashboard, add your own private connector.

In Claude Code, say **"connect my brain everywhere"** and follow the steps. It uses this button:

[![Deploy to Cloudflare](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/button)](https://deploy.workers.cloudflare.com/?url=https://github.com/msaifullahabdullah/agentic-os-starter/tree/main/connector)

You'll choose a password. It's free and runs on your own Cloudflare account.

## Things to say

| Say | What happens |
|---|---|
| "set me up" | The interview that fills your brain |
| "save this to my brain" | Files what you just said |
| "what does my brain know about…" | Answers from your brain |
| "publish my dashboard" | Makes or updates your dashboard |
| "set up my routines" | Morning briefing, evening check in, weekly clean up |
| "make me an agent for…" | Builds a new agent |
| "how do I work?" | An honest profile of your habits, after about 2 weeks |
| "connect my brain everywhere" | Use it in any Claude chat and on your phone |

## Your privacy

- Everything lives in **your** GitHub and **your** Cloudflare. Nothing is shared with whoever sent you this.
- It never stores passwords, card numbers or ID numbers.
- Nothing is sent to anyone, bought or booked without your yes.
