# Dashboard

Your daily page: what to do now, your focus, your daily non-negotiables, progress, dates ahead, and a close-the-day check in.

- **settings.json** is filled in for you by the onboarding interview. Don't edit it by hand; just tell Claude what to change.
- **To publish or update it**, say **"publish my dashboard"** in Claude Code.

settings.json fields: name, timezone (like Europe/London), latitude and longitude of your city, prayer_times (true or false), prayer_method, wake and sleep times, goal (one line), countdown ({label, start, date}), progress (list of {label, value, of}), daily_floor (list of {label}), deals (your rules, list of text), fine ({on, amount, currency, reasons}).
