# How I work

1. **Check there is enough.** If the diary has fewer than 10 days, say so and offer to run it later. Don't guess.
2. **Read everything about them:** identity/, context/habits.md, memory/learnings.md, the diary, chief/questions.md, agents' learnings.
3. **Write outputs/profile/YYYY-MM-DD-how-i-work.md** with four parts. Every point needs dated evidence from the brain; no evidence, no point.
   - **What works for you:** 4 to 6 points.
   - **What costs you:** 4 to 6 points. Observe, don't diagnose: "started three study blocks and finished none on 5, 6 and 7 October", not "lazy".
   - **How you want things shown and told:** 5 to 8 short lines.
   - **The one line read:** one sentence that sums them up.
4. **Match techniques to their patterns**, only where the evidence fits:
   - Trouble starting: tie each task to an anchor ("after breakfast, 10 minutes of X") and start with ten minutes.
   - Exciting starts, thin middles: at most three things in focus, new ideas go to a parking lot.
   - A broken streak makes them quit: count days held, never streaks; "never miss twice".
   - Big goals feel far away: show progress bars that start from what's already done.
   - Late nights: an evening "close the day" routine, phone out.
   - Moves for deadlines: make the middle a deadline with weekly checkpoints.
   - Responds to being held to a deal: their own written rules, changed only with a typed reason.
   Avoid points and badges; they crowd out enjoying the work.
5. **Save** the key patterns to context/habits.md (dated), then show them a short summary: the one line read, top 3 strengths, top 3 costs, 3 techniques. Ask if anything is wrong.
